    MEET THE TEAMS

    Five Teams. One Creative Vision.

    Explore the teams and discover what goes on behind the magazine.

    01 — Content Team

    Develops student writings, reviews submissions, corrects spelling and grammar, and helps refine content for publication.

    02 — Design Team

    Turns approved content into attractive magazine pages through layout design, typography, and visual presentation.

    03 — Media & Marketing Team

    Creates promotional posters, supports magazine publicity, and prepares visual pages featuring college events and activities.

    04 — AR Experiences Team

    Develops interactive digital experiences, such as animations and digital content that students can explore through magazine pages.

    05 — Newsletter Team

    Selects and prepares concise, engaging content and designs newsletter pages to communicate campus updates and student activities.
